Linear Classifier & Interpretability

1. Logistic Regression

Logistic Regression models the posterior probability of coronary heart disease via the logistic sigmoid function: P(Y=1|X) = 1 / (1 + e^-(β·X)). It calculates the log-odds of disease as a linear combination of normalized patient biomarkers.

  • L2 Regularization (Ridge): Applied with penalty C=0.8 to prevent coefficient divergence and multicollinearity among clinical parameters.
  • Odds Ratio Interpretability: Direct clinical explainability—positive coefficients indicate risk escalation while negative coefficients represent cardiac protection.
  • Calibrated Probabilities: Smooth continuous risk probability curve ideal for baseline clinical triage.
Kernel Margin Optimization

2. Support Vector Machine (SVM)

Support Vector Machines identify the optimal hyper-plane maximizing the geometric margin between healthy individuals and ischemic heart disease patients in a transformed Hilbert feature space.

  • Radial Basis Function (RBF) Kernel: Effectively projects nonlinear interactions (such as combined age, ST depression, and maximum heart rate) into higher dimensions.
  • Platt Probability Calibration: Wrapped in CalibratedClassifierCV to transform margin distances into rigorous posterior probabilities.
  • Robust to Outliers: Decision boundary is determined solely by critical support vectors rather than global cohort means.
Ensemble Decision Trees

3. Random Forest Classifier

Random Forest aggregates an ensemble of 150 randomized decision trees trained via bootstrap aggregating (bagging) and random feature subspace partitioning to minimize variance without increasing bias.

  • Superior Test Accuracy (90.16%): Highest performance on independent test data with an outstanding ROC-AUC of 0.9556.
  • Handles Complex Nonlinear Interactions: Captures hierarchical decision splits (e.g., asymptomatic chest pain paired with high cholesterol and low max heart rate).
  • Mean Decrease Impurity (MDI): Quantifies the diagnostic importance of each individual biomarker.
Medical Glossary

Clinical Biomarkers Reference

  • Thalach (Max Heart Rate): Reflects cardiovascular reserve. Chronotropic incompetence (inability to reach expected heart rate) is a prominent cardiac risk marker.
  • Oldpeak (ST Depression): Measures horizontal or downsloping ST-segment depression on stress ECG relative to rest. Values > 1.0 mm indicate myocardial ischemia.
  • CA (Fluoroscopy Vessels): Number of major epicardial coronary vessels (0–3) demonstrating >50% luminal narrowing on cardiac catheterization.
  • Thal (Thalassemia Scintigraphy): Perfusion scan assessing reversible ischemia (viable myocardium at risk) vs. fixed defect (previous myocardial infarction).
  • Exang (Exercise Angina): Provocation of ischemic chest pain during treadmill exertion.
